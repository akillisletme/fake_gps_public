# Features

The map screen has **three top-level modes**, switched by tapping the title bar:

```
FakeGPS (simulation)  ⇄  Tools (measure & analyse)  ⇄  Tracker (record real walks)
```

---

## FakeGPS — 5 Simulation Modes

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Fixed Location** | Tap anywhere on the map to instantly pin your location — no movement, no drift |
| **Joystick** | Move your location in real time with an on-screen joystick |
| **Route** | Drop 2 to 10 points and travel along them; pause / resume, save and reload |
| **Advanced Route** | Up to 10 waypoints with per-point speed, altitude, dwell time and signal-loss simulation; looping and a route queue |
| **Human Simulation** | Natural movement within a radius — Walking, Running, Cycling, Driving, or Stationary |

### Route tools

- Add, drag and reorder waypoints on the map
- Undo the last waypoint
- Save routes and reload them from the drawer
- Import routes from GPX, KML and TCX files; export as GPX

---

## Tracker — Record Your Real Walks

Record your actual GPS track, then replay it as a simulated route.

- **Keeps recording with the screen off**, with a notification you can always see
- Live stats while recording: duration, distance, current speed, GPS accuracy
- **Trim the start and end before saving**, so your home address stays out of the file
- Detail panel per walk: distance, duration, pace, date — rename, share or delete
- **Heatmap** — everywhere you have walked on one map; real walks and simulated
  routes are separate layers
- **Elevation profile** drawn from the recorded GPS altitude
- **History stats** — this week / this month / all time
- **Follow a saved track** — your phone vibrates if you drift off it
- **Export as GPX with timestamps** — opens as an activity in Strava or AllTrails
- Replay any recording as a simulated route with one tap

Recordings stay on your device.

## Tools — 10 Map Tools

| Tool | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Ruler** | Multi-point cumulative distance with on-map labels |
| **Area** | Polygon area *and* perimeter |
| **Circle** | Center + radius; circumference and area |
| **Bearing** | Azimuth, reverse azimuth and distance between two points |
| **Compass** | Live magnetometer heading with calibration warnings |
| **Cooldown** | Distance between two points → safe waiting time |
| **Sun** | Sunrise / sunset / day length, golden and blue hour |
| **Safe Radius** | The circle you can safely reach, growing over time |
| **Sun Path** | Day scrubber with an animated solar azimuth ray |
| **Shadow** | Object height + time of day → shadow length and direction |

- Drag any point to fine-tune a measurement
- Star your favourite tools for quick access
- **Metric, imperial or nautical units** — switchable from Settings

## Smart Features

- **Location search** (geocoding), manual coordinate entry, favourites
- **Photo GPS Editor** — view, edit or add EXIF GPS data; saves a **new copy**,
  your original gallery item is never edited in place
- **Coordinate Converter** — DD ↔ DMS ↔ UTM, fully offline
- **Scheduler** — have a location switch on at a time you choose; one-time or
  recurring, survives a device reboot
- **Smart Shield** — assign locations to specific apps; your location switches
  automatically when you open them
- **Home screen widget** — start a saved location with one tap
- **Floating widget** — control your GPS from a panel on top of any app
- **Deep links** (`fakegps://`) for locations, routes and simulations

## Free and Premium

All 5 simulation modes, walk recording and all 10 map tools are **free to use
within a daily allowance**. Premium removes the daily allowance and unlocks the
extra features listed below, and is available as a monthly or yearly
subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

Premium-only:

- Satellite, hybrid and terrain map types
- Traffic layer and the on-map compass
- Smart Shield, home screen widget, floating widget
- Scheduler, Photo GPS Editor, Coordinate Converter

There are **no ads** in either tier.

## Permissions

- **Location** — to centre the map, verify your mock-location setup, and (only
  while you are recording a walk you started yourself) sample your real GPS track
- **Usage access** *(optional, off by default)* — Smart Shield reads only the
  foreground app's package name to apply the profile you assigned to it. It does
  not read screen content and collects no personal data. Requires an explicit
  in-app disclosure and consent, and can be revoked anytime in Android settings.
